About Me

I'm an aspiring data analyst who learns by shipping dashboards, not just reading about them. Each project below started as a messy export — order logs, engagement exports, sales sheets — and ended as a small set of numbers someone could actually act on.

I work comfortably across the analyst stack: cleaning and querying data with Python, Pandas and SQL, then building the story on top in Power BI, Tableau or Excel. I care about dashboards that answer a question in one glance, not ones that just look busy.
